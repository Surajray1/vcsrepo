Detailed Daily Itinerary

Day 1: Copenhagen 🇩🇰 (Arrival)

Morning (7:00 AM): Wake up, arrive in Copenhagen, drop bags at the hotel.
Day: Explore Nyhavn, Rosenborg Castle, and Strøget.
Evening: Dinner, followed by a visit to Tivoli Gardens. Head back to the hotel by 11:00 PM to rest.

Day 2: Hamburg 🇩🇪 (Travel Day)

Morning (7:00 AM): Wake up, have breakfast, and check out of your hotel.
Train: Take an ~8:00 AM train from Copenhagen to Hamburg (arrives ~1:00 PM).
Afternoon: Check into your Hamburg hotel (after 2:00 PM). Explore the Speicherstadt.
Evening: Dinner in HafenCity. Back to hotel by 11:30 PM.

Day 3: Amsterdam 🇳🇱 (Travel Day)

Morning (6:30 AM): Wake up early, have breakfast, and check out of your hotel.
Train: Take a ~7:30 AM train from Hamburg to Amsterdam (arrives ~1:00 PM).
Afternoon: Drop bags at the hotel, have lunch, and take a canal cruise. Check in after 3:00 PM.
Evening: Explore and dine in the Jordaan district.

Day 4: Amsterdam 🇳🇱 (Full Day)

Morning (7:00 AM): Wake up and visit the Rijksmuseum or Van Gogh Museum.
Afternoon: Visit the Anne Frank House (pre-booked) and explore the 9 Streets.
Evening: Enjoy a traditional Dutch dinner.

Day 5: Brussels 🇧🇪 (Travel Day)

Morning (8:00 AM): Leisurely wake up, check out by 10:00 AM.
Train: Take a ~11:00 AM train from Amsterdam to Brussels (arrives ~1:00 PM).
Afternoon: Check into your hotel. Walk to the Grand-Place and see the Manneken Pis.
Evening: Indulge in mussels, waffles, and chocolate.

Day 6: Luxembourg 🇱🇺 (Travel Day)

Morning (8:00 AM): Wake up, check out of your hotel.
Train: Take a ~10:00 AM train from Brussels to Luxembourg (arrives ~1:00 PM).
Afternoon: Check in and explore the Bock Casemates and Chemin de la Corniche.
Evening: Dinner in the Grund district.

Day 7: Basel 🇨🇭 (Travel Day)

Morning (7:30 AM): Wake up, check out of your hotel.
Train: Take a ~9:00 AM train from Luxembourg to Basel (arrives ~1:00 PM).
Afternoon: Check into your hotel and explore Basel's Old Town and the Rhine.
Evening: Enjoy a hearty Swiss dinner.

Day 8: Basel 🇨🇭 (Full Day)

Morning (7:00 AM): Wake up for a full day in Switzerland. Consider a short trip to see nearby nature or visit the Basel Minster and local museums.
Afternoon: Continue exploring the city's art scene or relax by the river.
Evening: Enjoy a final Swiss meal.

Day 9: Paris 🇫🇷 (Travel Day)

Morning (8:00 AM): Enjoy a final Swiss breakfast and check out.
Train: Take a ~10:30 AM train from Basel to Paris (arrives ~1:30 PM).
Afternoon: Check into your hotel near Gare de Lyon.
Evening: Head to Trocadéro for Eiffel Tower views, then take a Seine cruise.

Days 10-11: Paris 🇫🇷 (Full Days & Departure)

Day 10: Wake up at 7:00 AM. Visit the Louvre, walk the Champs-Élysées to the Arc de Triomphe, and have dinner in Montmartre.
Day 11: Wake up at 7:00 AM. See Notre-Dame and Sainte-Chapelle. Enjoy a final lunch before heading to the airport for departure.
